# Code of Conduct - Logihertz Systems (OPC) Private Limited

## Core Values & Community Standards

Logihertz Systems (OPC) Private Limited is dedicated to fostering a professional, respectful, and harassment-free development environment across all projects in the Logihertz ecosystem.

We expect all contributors, reviewers, and maintainers to:
- Communicate with empathy, professional courtesy, and constructive mutual respect.
- Uphold technical excellence, cryptographic safety, and rigorous verification in hardware security engineering.
- Focus on objective, evidence-based architectural dialogue and collaborative problem-solving.

## Prohibited Behaviors

The following conduct is unacceptable across all community spaces and communication channels:
- Offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory language and personal attacks.
- Trolling, harassment, or public disruption of technical discussions and code reviews.
- Publishing or redistributing non-public personal information without authorization.
- Promoting deliberately compromised or backdoored hardware provisioning logic.

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of inappropriate or abusive behavior may be reported directly to:
- **Leadership Contact**: Sameer Tawade <ops@logihertz.com>

Project maintainers will investigate all reports promptly and impartially, taking necessary corrective actions up to and including permanent exclusion from project repositories.
